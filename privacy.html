Data practices

Privacy

Your library stays on your devices and, if you connect it, in your own Google Drive.

What Music accesses

Music reads audio you import and the details you enter or receive with a source: song title, artist, source channel and video links, duration, filename, audio format and integrity checksums. It uses your connected Google account’s email address to confirm the chosen account and storage information to support the Drive connection.

How Google Drive is used

With your consent, Music uploads audio and library metadata to an app-created Danny Music folder, reads its recovery snapshots, and downloads songs for local playback. The requested Google permission is drive.file: files created by this app or explicitly chosen for use with it. Music does not request full-Drive access.

Audio objects and dated library snapshots are retained so a device can recover the library. Changing a title does not replace the original audio. Sync does not automatically delete old cloud copies.

Storage and sharing

Audio and library details are stored locally. The optional remote copy is stored by Google Drive in your account. OAuth credentials are stored in the system Keychain on the connected device and are excluded from library backups.

Music has no developer-operated storage server, advertising service, analytics collection, or AI training pipeline. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or sent to an AI model. The app does not share your Drive files publicly or with another account.

When you request a YouTube import, Music contacts YouTube for that source. It does not send your Drive credentials or library to YouTube. Google and YouTube operate under their own applicable policies.

Your controls

You can disconnect Drive in Music to stop syncing and remove the app’s locally saved connection credentials. Downloaded songs and existing cloud backups remain. You can separately revoke the app’s access in your Google Account and manage or remove its cloud files in Google Drive.

Portable backups and retained Drive snapshots contain library metadata and audio. Keep or remove those copies according to your own recovery needs. This website does not use analytics or cookies added by this project; its hosting provider may process ordinary web requests.

Google API data use

Danny Music’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the visible sync, offline playback, and recovery features described here.

Contact

The project’s support contact is listed on its Google consent screen. This personal app is not offered as a public service.
