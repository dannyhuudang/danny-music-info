Danny Music is a personal music player that keeps a complete local library, with optional Google Drive sync and recovery.

Listen your way

Browse one library by song or artist, edit track details, and use shuffle and repeat-song together. Imported audio stays on the device for offline playback.

A recovery copy you control

When connected, Music saves audio and dated library snapshots in a private Danny Music folder in your Google Drive. It downloads missing songs to the other connected device and checks file integrity before accepting them.

The Google connection requests access only to files created by Music or explicitly selected for it. It does not request access to your entire Drive. Credentials stay in the device’s system Keychain.

About this project

This is a personal prototype, not a public download or subscription service. Sync currently runs while Music is open. No paid storage plan is activated by the app.
