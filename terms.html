Use of the app

Danny Music is a personal software project for organizing and playing a locally retained music library.

Availability

The prototype is supplied for personal testing. It is not a public app download, commercial service, or subscription. Features and compatibility may change as development continues.

Your files and account

Import only content you are entitled to store and use. You retain control of your audio, metadata, Google account, and storage. Connecting Drive enables the file transfers described in the app and in the privacy information.

Third-party services

Google Drive, YouTube, Apple installation and device services have their own terms, limits, and availability. Music does not activate a paid storage plan or collect subscription payments. Any separate service plan is your choice.

Recovery and limitations

File checks and dated snapshots help with recovery, but this prototype does not promise uninterrupted availability or recovery from every failure. Keep an independent copy of irreplaceable files while testing. Sync currently needs the app to remain open for transfers to finish.

Stopping use

You can stop using the app and disconnect its Google connection at any time. Existing Drive files and downloaded music remain until you choose to remove them.
